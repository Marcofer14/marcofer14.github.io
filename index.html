01 / Reinforcement learningFlagship

Competitive Pokémon RL agents

Agents that play competitive Gen 9 VGC doubles on Pokémon Showdown. Three model families — search, policy gradient and game theory — trained and benchmarked against each other.

  • Custom Gymnasium environment for Gen 9 doubles on top of poke-env
  • Real Showdown server and simulator served from Docker for training and search
  • Random and greedy baselines, reproducible presets, smoke tests and model cards
3 trained modelshead-to-head tournament
i
AlphaZero-style MCTSSearch + PPO

Policy/value network guides tree search over legal double actions; branches are played out on the real Showdown engine. Pretrained on replays, then updated with PPO on visit counts.

ii
Recurrent PPOPolicy gradient

Memory-based policy that tracks the battle across turns, trained with a curriculum and a self-play league of past checkpoints.

iii
CFR + neural priorGame theory

Approximate counterfactual regret minimization over public-state abstractions; a neural prior generalizes to states the regret table rarely visits.
